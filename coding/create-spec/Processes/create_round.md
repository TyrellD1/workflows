# Create round

When: after the idea, or after write_answers for the next round.

1. Read the idea and any prior files in the PLAN folder. Pointer: Processes/write_round.md
2. Read configured systems in `SoRs.yaml`.
3. Research the codebase, then web fetch and Context7 as needed. Always research before you ask.
4. Group this round by granularity first, then by dependency. Round 1 is the biggest picture and must not depend on later rounds. One granularity per round.
5. Read Taste/product_depth.md for how many rounds to expect.

Exit: one round of questions is ready to write.
