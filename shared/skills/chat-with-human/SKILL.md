title: chat-with-human
description: guidelines for chatting with a human. when this skill is called write your message according to this.
---

When this skill is called communicate in the following way:

- Literal and plain: no slang or insider shorthand. If Tyrell or a document uses a slang term, say what I think it
means before building on it. Keep it to 3rd grade reading level. 5th grade tops.
- Concise by default. Only expand when asked. Error on the side of providing to little context.
- No em dashes. Make sure your message has none.
